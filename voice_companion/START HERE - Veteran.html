Current Keyboard And Voice Input

The browser entry box and Ctrl+L accept an address such as example.com without www or a scheme. You can also type www.example.com, https://example.com or http://example.com; HTTP is upgraded to HTTPS. Paths, queries and fragments are retained. Words without an address perform a search. Website addresses, email addresses and passwords can be dictated or typed. Say addresses using at, dot and slash as needed. Password values remain masked in page text and review feedback. You may also speak search terms, saved contact names and website favorites.

Dynamic rendered pages update the virtual viewer while using only the keyboard. Quick-key heading and link navigation moves the same reading caret used by arrows. Arriving at a list item speaks that item. An additional attempt to move beyond the first or last item announces Start of list or End of list. Home and End announce the destination item when moving; pressing the same key again at its boundary announces Start of list or End of list.

Voice commands use the default microphone selected in Windows. The external-only detection safeguard has been removed because it could reject working headsets. USB, USB-C, Bluetooth, analog jack and internal microphones can be used when Windows provides a usable input. If the microphone cannot open, keyboard navigation remains available. Check Windows input settings and microphone permissions, then restart Companion.

Start

  1. Open Voice Companion from the desktop icon or Start menu. A normal Voice Companion window shows the current prompt and recent responses. The app also speaks them aloud without JAWS or NVDA. The app checks for updates first. If an update is available, the microphone opens for your Yes or No answer. Otherwise wait for "Voice Companion," the version number, "is ready. Say wake up to control the app by voice, or use your Up and Down arrow keys, Enter and Escape to move through the menus. Help is available." If you hear an older version or "email connection is still to come," you opened an old EXE. Ask your trainer to run the new Setup file and use its desktop shortcut.
  2. Say: "Wake up."
  3. Wait for "Main menu. What would you like to do?" Then say what you want. Say "Next" or "Previous" to browse Word processor, Email, Web browser, Radio, Podcasts, Notepad, Help, What's new, Options, Check for updates, Restart computer, Shut down computer, and Exit Voice Companion; say "That one," "Confirm that," "Okay," or "OK" to open the announced option. Say Word processor or Notepad to open those activities directly. Direct commands such as "Write a document," "Create a document," and "Email" still work. Say "What version?" at any time to confirm the current test build.

Using The User Guide

After checking for updates, Companion says its version and that it is ready, followed by "Say wake up to control the app by voice, or use your Up and Down arrow keys, Enter and Escape to move through the menus. Help is available." After you wake it, say "Help" to open this same complete guide inside the app. In the guide, say "Next" or "Previous" to hear one topic at a time, then "That one," "Okay," or "Confirm" to open it. Larger topics such as email and documents have subtopics. Radio presets, podcast playback, and email list size have separate topics. Move through them the same way, then say "That one" to read one. Say "Continue reading" for more, or "Read from top," "Next word," "Previous sentence," "Next paragraph," and the other reading commands to review. Say "Back to subsections," "Back to topics," or "Back to email topics" to navigate. Say "Close help" to return to what you were doing. Say "Main menu" to leave help and return to the start. To open a topic directly, say "Help with" followed by its name, such as "Help with documents," "Help with email," "Help with web," "Help with radio," "Help with presets," or "Help with podcasts."

Deeper Web And Media Tasks

In an editable web field, voice dictation now inserts where spoken character or word navigation placed the caret. Say Select all, Read selection, or Scratch to select, review or remove staged text. Dictation replaces selected text. Say Save field to apply the changes or Cancel field editing to leave the existing page value unchanged. Read-only fields identify themselves and do not accept dictation.

Say Fill out this form for guided entry. Disabled and read-only fields are skipped. Labelled rich text fields and custom choice controls are included, and newly revealed conditional questions are checked after each answer. If a form changes ambiguously, Companion stops and asks you to read the page again. Passwords can be dictated; file uploads still need manual input. Website verification and CAPTCHA can still prevent a completely hands-free task.

Say Close list to leave a web field or favorite list and return to the page. Radio and podcast result lists announce their first or last boundary on entry. If audio cannot start or resume within twelve seconds, Companion returns an error so you can choose another station or episode.

Starting an email by voice still asks who to send it to, then guides you through the subject and message. The automatic draft name and sending account are not read when creating the message.

Lists announce "Start of list," "End of list," or "Start and end of list" for a single item before optional instructions. These announcements remain at high, medium, and low verbosity. Entering an inbox and entering a settings choice also announce their boundaries. Home and End move to the first and last item. If an email page cannot load, your existing messages and position remain available so you can retry. When leaving a named document, answering Yes to save keeps its filename and closes the editor. Untitled documents still ask for a filename. A failed save leaves the document open for another attempt.

Speech Controls

While the app is talking, say "Be quiet," "Stop," "Shut up," "Hush up," "Shut the hell up," or "Shut the fuck up." It stops speaking without answering back. Say "Resume," "Keep going," or "Keep talking" to continue at the same place. You can also say another command, such as "Next message," to stop the current reading and do that instead. These controls work in email, documents, web pages, and other parts of the app. A headset helps the microphone distinguish your voice from the app's voice.

Say "Faster" or "Slower" to change speaking speed. Say "Louder," "Quieter," or "Softer" to change speech volume. Softer does exactly the same thing as Quieter. For an exact speech rate, say "Speed 2," "Rate 2," or "Set speech rate to 2." The range is minus ten through ten; zero is the normal rate. Say "Rate minus two" for a slower setting. For exact volume, say "Volume 50" or "Set volume to 50." Volume ranges from zero to one hundred percent; "Volume 5" means five percent. These controls work with SAPI speech, eSpeak, and configured AI speech throughout the app, including help and the tutorial. They keep your chosen synthesizer and voice. Say "Voice list" or "List voices" to select a synthesizer first: SAPI speech, eSpeak NG, or AI voice when configured. Say "Next" or "Previous," then "Okay," "Confirm that," or "That one" to choose the synthesizer. While browsing synthesizers, Companion briefly says the option number and synthesizer name. Next, hear its first voice. Use the same commands to choose a voice and keep it. "Next voice" and "Previous voice" still work. Say "Cancel voice" at either stage to restore your previous choice. Say "Select voice" to choose another voice in your current synthesizer. eSpeak offers six simple choices: US English, UK English, US English Male, UK English Male, US English Female, and UK English Female. Use Next or Previous to preview them, or say "Use voice eSpeak US English Female" directly. Narration needs no internet or API credits. You can also say "Use voice" followed by a full voice name. Say "Punctuation none," "Punctuation some," or "Punctuation all." The app remembers these choices for the next time you open it. Say "Check voices" to save a voice check for your helper. This diagnostic lists Windows SAPI 5 voices; it does not test AI voices. Legacy SAPI 4 voices and some Windows Narrator natural voices do not appear in the SAPI 5 picker.

If it does not hear you, speak toward the computer and pause briefly between requests. A helper can check that Windows allows the program to use the microphone and that the correct microphone is selected.

If it says speech files are missing or it cannot use the microphone, ask your helper to run check-setup.cmd in the same folder. If it does not speak at all, ask your helper to check the computer's sound and run check-setup.cmd.

Keep the entire VoiceCompanion folder together. If the helper's check says VoiceCompanion.exe is missing, ask for the complete program folder.

Ai Voices

After your trainer completes AI voice setup, say "List voices." Choose the AI voice synthesizer using Next or Previous and Okay. Then say "Next voice" or "Previous voice" to hear a sample. AI choices announce their names as "AI voice" followed by the name. When you hear the voice you want, say "Okay," "That one," or "Confirm that." Companion saves the selection for the next launch. Say "Cancel voice" to restore your previous selection. Selecting a SAPI voice switches back to SAPI speech automatically.

AI narration applies throughout Companion, including the tutorial, documents, email, web reading, and help. It requires internet access and a funded OpenAI API account. The text being read is sent to OpenAI. Generating a sample may take several seconds. Speech rate, volume, punctuation, Be quiet, and Resume speaking also apply to AI speech.

If AI narration fails, Companion attempts to read the same text using SAPI speech. This fallback still needs testing on the client's Windows computer. If there are no AI choices in List voices, ask your trainer to complete setup. The voice list shows configured choices, not a guarantee that the API account has credits or that every voice will work.

For your trainer: say "Set up AI voice" or choose the "AI voice setup (trainer)" button. Enter the API key in the hidden field, choose a voice, confirm permission to send narration text to OpenAI, and select "Save AI voice settings." Then use List voices to preview and select a voice. Cancel or Escape closes setup without saving. "Remove saved AI settings" deletes the saved credential and returns to SAPI speech. A separately configured Windows environment variable must be removed separately. API usage is billed separately from ChatGPT. The saved key is protected by the Windows user account.

Voice Input Modes

Normal mode is on when the app opens. It recognizes commands and adds ordinary speech to an open document, email draft, note, or a focused text field in the guided browser. You never need to say start or stop dictation.

Say "Commands only" when you want to navigate or review without adding ordinary speech as text. Say "Dictation only" when you want everything you say to become text in the current document, draft, note, or focused browser text field. In dictation only, even words like "send it" are typed; say "Normal mode" to use commands again. You can always say "What mode?" to hear the current setting. Sleep and shut down commands still work.

In the browser, select a normal text field before speaking into it. Email addresses, website addresses and passwords accept dictation. Number and code fields may need precise keyboard entry. Review text and form answers before submitting.

Reading Text

In a document, email draft, open message, web page, or note, say "Start reading" or "Read from top" to hear text from the beginning. Say "Continue reading" for more. Say "Next character," "Previous character," "Next word," "Previous word," "Next line," "Previous line," "Next sentence," "Previous sentence," "Next paragraph," or "Previous paragraph" to move one piece at a time. These are text lines and paragraphs as read by the app; they may differ from visual lines on a screen. In dictation only, switch to normal mode before giving reading commands.

Contacts are kept separately for each selected email account. With Gmail selected, say "Connect Google Contacts" or "Sync Google Contacts" to open the normal Google permission screen and exchange contacts. A trusted helper can complete that permission step. Saved local contacts remain available without internet.

To type or correct an address, say "Go to To," "Go to CC," or "Go to BCC." The typing box becomes ready automatically. When returning to To, your current address appears for editing. Left and Right Arrow review characters aloud; Backspace and Delete remove characters and announce the deletion. New characters are spoken as you type using your local synthesizer: eSpeak when selected, otherwise SAPI speech, including when an AI narration voice is selected. Quick Test opens the typing window too. Press Enter to save the corrected address. When a new email opens, To is ready automatically too. The To field can also be edited in forward, reply, and reply-all drafts; after recording an address in a response draft, Companion returns to the message body. Repeated prompts do not erase an address you are typing. Alt+T is only a fallback if another window has taken keyboard focus. Say "Email to friend@example.com" with the full email address. Say "Read recipient" to check it.

Type "Email to friend@example.com" in the command box, or type the address directly into To. Website and email addresses and passwords cannot be dictated. Saved contact names and website favorites can still be selected by voice.

Say "Subject is Hello from me." Say "Read subject" to check it.

These same select, find, bold, underline, italicize, highlight, copy, cut, paste, delete, and scratch that commands work in the email message body. Font, font size, alignment, headings one through six, line spacing, bulleted lists, numbered lists, and tables also work there, including in dictation-only mode. Use the document commands above. Formatting is preserved in saved drafts and outgoing Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo messages, including replies and forwards. The receiving email program may display fonts and spacing differently. Selection commands also work in To, CC, BCC, and Subject; these fields contain plain text. In the typing window, selection uses your current entry, including characters you have not submitted yet. Say "What is selected" to hear it. Type to replace selected text, then press Enter to record the field.

After the recipient and subject, just speak your message. The app adds ordinary speech to the draft and handles short commands such as "Read paragraph," "Undo," and "New paragraph." You do not need to start or stop dictation. The app turns spoken "period," "comma," "question mark," and "exclamation point" into punctuation marks within or at the end of an utterance, including when the next word is lowercase and removes a stray final "uh" or "um." Listen back before sending. To put exact command or punctuation words into your message, say "Type literally" followed by those words. Say "Save draft" when finished. Say "Cancel" or "Back to inbox" to discard the draft. Say "Save draft" to keep it before leaving; "Exit" or "Leave email" saves it and returns to the main menu.

You can say "Create a document" or "Write a note" directly while in an email draft. The draft is saved before the new task starts.

Later, say "List email drafts" and "Open email draft Untitled" (or the draft name you heard) to return to one after restarting.

If it says a saved draft could not be opened, ask your trainer for help. The app leaves that saved file untouched.

With a connected account, say "Send it" or "Send that." Listen to the sender, recipient, subject, and whole message. Say "Cancel send" if anything is wrong. Say "Yes" only when all of it is correct. It finishes that announcement before refreshing the folder you were using. For a reply or forward, it returns to the original message if that message is still on the refreshed page; otherwise it returns to the folder list. Ask your helper to verify delivery during early tests. If the app says the result is uncertain, check with the recipient and your mail account before trying again.

Read And Organize Email - Helper Must Connect And Test The Account First

Say "Email" or "Check email" for the selected inbox. You may also say "Check Gmail," "Check Outlook," or "Check Yahoo" when exactly one account of that type is connected. The app reads a numbered list in Inbox. For each message, it says only the sender, subject, and date. Say "Read message 1" or "Open message 1" to hear its body immediately. Say "Message details" if you want the sender, subject, and date repeated. For a long message, say "Continue reading" until you hear "End of message." Say "Read from beginning" to start it again. Say "Next" or "Previous" (or "Next message" and "Previous message") to hear one short message summary at a time.
