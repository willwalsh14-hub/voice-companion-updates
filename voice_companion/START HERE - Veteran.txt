VOICE COMPANION - SIMPLE TEST GUIDE

Guide build: 0.2.97-test. This is an early test program. A helper double-clicks VoiceCompanion-Setup.exe once to install it. This is the Setup file from the builder folder, not the small source ZIP or a temporary application EXE. On the final Setup page, "Open Voice Companion now" is checked by default. Leave it checked and select Finish to open the app. A helper may uncheck it to open the app later. There are no separate Python or speech-model installs on your computer. It speaks aloud and listens through the computer's microphone. You do not need Microsoft Word to make a document.

START

1. Open Voice Companion from the desktop icon or Start menu. A normal Voice Companion window shows the current prompt and recent responses. The app also speaks them aloud without JAWS or NVDA. The app checks for updates first. If an update is available, the microphone opens for your Yes or No answer. Otherwise wait for "Voice Companion," the version number, "is ready. Say wake up to get started and launch the main menu. Say help for the user guide." If you hear an older version or "email connection is still to come," you opened an old EXE. Ask your trainer to run the new Setup file and use its desktop shortcut.
2. Say: "Wake up."
3. Wait for "Main menu. What would you like to do?" Then say what you want. Say "Next" or "Previous" to browse Documents, Email, Web browsing, Radio, Podcasts, Notes, Help, and Exit Voice Companion; say "That one," "Confirm that," "Okay," or "OK" to open the announced option. Direct commands such as "Write a document," "Create a document," and "Email" still work. Say "What version?" at any time to confirm the current test build.

USING THE USER GUIDE

After checking for updates, Companion says its version and that it is ready, followed by "Say wake up to get started and launch the main menu. Say help for the user guide." After you wake it, say "Help" to open this same complete guide inside the app. In the guide, say "Next" or "Previous" to hear one topic at a time, then "That one," "Okay," or "Confirm" to open it. Larger topics such as email and documents have subtopics. Radio presets, podcast playback, and email list size have separate topics. Move through them the same way, then say "That one" to read one. Say "Continue reading" for more, or "Read from top," "Next word," "Previous sentence," "Next paragraph," and the other reading commands to review. Say "Back to subsections," "Back to topics," or "Back to email topics" to navigate. Say "Close help" to return to what you were doing. Say "Main menu" to leave help and return to the start. To open a topic directly, say "Help with" followed by its name, such as "Help with documents," "Help with email," "Help with web," "Help with radio," "Help with presets," or "Help with podcasts."

COMMANDS AND MODES

Say "Help" at any time after waking Companion to browse every guide topic, one at a time. Say "Help with commands" to return here. Say "Help with" followed by a topic name. For example, say "Help with documents" or "Help with presets." Say "Back to topics" for the full list and "Close help" to return to your task.
Across Companion, say "Next" or "Previous" while browsing a list, then "That one," "Okay," or "Confirm" to choose the item. Say "Go back" to leave the current view, or "Main menu" to return to the start. Say "Be quiet" to stop Companion speech and "Resume" to continue. Say "Go to sleep" to pause requests while keeping your current folder, message, document, or draft. Say "Wake up" to continue in the same place. Sleep cancels a pending send confirmation; review it again before sending. Say "Shut down Companion" to close it. For reading text, say "Read from top," "Continue reading," "Next word," "Previous line," or "Next paragraph."

SPEECH CONTROLS

While the app is talking, say "Be quiet," "Stop," "Shut up," "Hush up," "Shut the hell up," or "Shut the fuck up." It stops speaking without answering back. Say "Resume," "Keep going," or "Keep talking" to continue at the same place. You can also say another command, such as "Next message," to stop the current reading and do that instead. These controls work in email, documents, web pages, and other parts of the app. A headset helps the microphone distinguish your voice from the app's voice.
Say "Faster" or "Slower" to change speaking speed. Say "Louder," "Quieter," or "Softer" to change speech volume. Softer does exactly the same thing as Quieter. For an exact speech rate, say "Speed 2," "Rate 2," or "Set speech rate to 2." The range is minus ten through ten; zero is the normal rate. Say "Rate minus two" for a slower setting. For exact volume, say "Volume 50" or "Set volume to 50." Volume ranges from zero to one hundred percent; "Volume 5" means five percent. These controls work with Windows speech, eSpeak, and configured AI speech throughout the app, including help and the tutorial. They keep your chosen synthesizer and voice. Say "Voice list" or "List voices" to select a synthesizer first: Windows speech, eSpeak NG, or AI voice when configured. Say "Next" or "Previous," then "Okay," "Confirm that," or "That one" to choose the synthesizer. While browsing synthesizers, Companion briefly says the option number and synthesizer name. Next, hear its first voice. Use the same commands to choose a voice and keep it. "Next voice" and "Previous voice" still work. Say "Cancel voice" at either stage to restore your previous choice. Say "Select voice" to choose another voice in your current synthesizer. eSpeak offers six simple choices: US English, UK English, US English Male, UK English Male, US English Female, and UK English Female. Use Next or Previous to preview them, or say "Use voice eSpeak US English Female" directly. Narration needs no internet or API credits. You can also say "Use voice" followed by a full voice name. Say "Punctuation none," "Punctuation some," or "Punctuation all." The app remembers these choices for the next time you open it. Say "Check voices" to save a voice check for your helper. This diagnostic lists Windows SAPI 5 voices; it does not test AI voices. Legacy SAPI 4 voices and some Windows Narrator natural voices do not appear in the SAPI 5 picker.
If it does not hear you, speak toward the computer and pause briefly between requests. A helper can check that Windows allows the program to use the microphone and that the correct microphone is selected.
If it says speech files are missing or it cannot use the microphone, ask your helper to run check-setup.cmd in the same folder. If it does not speak at all, ask your helper to check the computer's sound and run check-setup.cmd.
Keep the entire VoiceCompanion folder together. If the helper's check says VoiceCompanion.exe is missing, ask for the complete program folder.

AI VOICES

After your trainer completes AI voice setup, say "List voices." Choose the AI voice synthesizer using Next or Previous and Okay. Then say "Next voice" or "Previous voice" to hear a sample. AI choices announce their names as "AI voice" followed by the name. When you hear the voice you want, say "Okay," "That one," or "Confirm that." Companion saves the selection for the next launch. Say "Cancel voice" to restore your previous selection. Selecting a Windows voice switches back to Windows speech automatically.
AI narration applies throughout Companion, including the tutorial, documents, email, web reading, and help. It requires internet access and a funded OpenAI API account. The text being read is sent to OpenAI. Generating a sample may take several seconds. Speech rate, volume, punctuation, Be quiet, and Resume speaking also apply to AI speech.
If AI narration fails, Companion attempts to read the same text using Windows speech. This fallback still needs testing on the client's Windows computer. If there are no AI choices in List voices, ask your trainer to complete setup. The voice list shows configured choices, not a guarantee that the API account has credits or that every voice will work.
For your trainer: say "Set up AI voice" or choose the "AI voice setup (trainer)" button. Enter the API key in the hidden field, choose a voice, confirm permission to send narration text to OpenAI, and select "Save AI voice settings." Then use List voices to preview and select a voice. Cancel or Escape closes setup without saving. "Remove saved AI settings" deletes the saved credential and returns to Windows speech. A separately configured Windows environment variable must be removed separately. API usage is billed separately from ChatGPT. The saved key is protected by the Windows user account.

PRACTICE TUTORIAL

After waking Companion, say "Start tutorial." The spoken tutorial asks you to choose "Practice radio," "Practice email," or "Practice documents." It gives you one instruction at a time and listens for your answer. Say "Repeat" to hear the last instruction again. Say "Main menu" to leave. You can start another lesson whenever you want.
In radio practice, say "Search WGN," then "Add preset," "Add favorite," "Save preset," or "Save favorite." Say "List presets," "That one," and "Delete preset." In email practice, say "Go to inbox," "Next message," "Select" or "Mark," "Delete," and then "Yes" or "No." In document practice, say "Write a document," "Hello world," and "Read from top."
These are fictional practice items. The tutorial does not sign in, send or delete real mail, save real presets, open a radio stream, or create a document. Its narration uses the selected app voice. Windows speech is the default. Say List voices, choose a synthesizer with Next or Previous and Okay, then choose its voice the same way. eSpeak has six choices: US English, UK English, US English Male, UK English Male, US English Female, and UK English Female; AI requires setup. Say Cancel voice to restore the previous selection.

VOICE INPUT MODES

Normal mode is on when the app opens. It recognizes commands and adds ordinary speech to an open document, email draft, note, or a focused text field in the guided browser. You never need to say start or stop dictation.
Say "Commands only" when you want to navigate or review without adding ordinary speech as text. Say "Dictation only" when you want everything you say to become text in the current document, draft, note, or focused browser text field. In dictation only, even words like "send it" are typed; say "Normal mode" to use commands again. You can always say "What mode?" to hear the current setting. Sleep and shut down commands still work.
In the browser, select a normal text field before speaking into it. Passwords and precise address, number, and code fields need private keyboard entry. Review text and form answers before submitting.

READING TEXT

In a document, email draft, open message, web page, or note, say "Start reading" or "Read from top" to hear text from the beginning. Say "Continue reading" for more. Say "Next character," "Previous character," "Next word," "Previous word," "Next line," "Previous line," "Next sentence," "Previous sentence," "Next paragraph," or "Previous paragraph" to move one piece at a time. These are text lines and paragraphs as read by the app; they may differ from visual lines on a screen. In dictation only, switch to normal mode before giving reading commands.

WRITE A DOCUMENT

Say "Create a document."
Say "Name document My Letter."
Speak a sentence or two at a time. The app adds ordinary speech to your document.
Say "New paragraph" when you want a new paragraph. You can give editing commands without pausing dictation.
Say "Read paragraph" to hear the current paragraph.
Say "New line" to move to the next line within the same paragraph. Say "New paragraph" to start a separate paragraph. Say "Next paragraph" or "Previous paragraph" to move.
Say "Next sentence," "Previous sentence," "Next word," or "Spell word" to review.
Say "Go to beginning of document," "Move to beginning of document," "Go to end of document," or "Move to end of document." Moving clears selected text, including a previous Select all. Later heading or alignment changes apply at your new paragraph. Next and previous word, line, sentence, paragraph, heading, and table-cell commands also clear selections. These movement commands work in document and email-body dictation-only mode too.
After moving to a word, say "Replace word with" and the new word, or "Delete word."
After moving to a sentence, say "Replace sentence with" and the new sentence, or "Delete sentence."
Say "Change hello to goodbye" or "Replace hello with goodbye" to search the whole document or email body. One match is replaced immediately. For multiple matches, hear a brief sentence excerpt and browse with Next or Previous, then That one, Confirm that, or OK. Cancel replacement leaves the text unchanged. Replacement ignores punctuation added at the end of the command; say "period" or another punctuation name to include it deliberately.
Say "Replace paragraph with" followed by the new paragraph to rewrite it. Say "Insert before red fox" or "Insert after red fox" to place the writing cursor. Repeated targets use the same match browsing. After confirming the target, dictate what to insert, or say "Type literally" followed by the text in commands-only mode.
Say "Select word," "Select line," "Select sentence," "Select character," or "Select paragraph" to select the current unit. Say "Select all" for the whole document. Say "Select first paragraph," "Select next paragraph," "Select previous paragraph," or "Select paragraph 3." First, next, previous, last, current, and numbered selection commands also work for characters, words, lines, and sentences within the current paragraph. Lines use explicit line breaks. Say "Select cat," "Find the phrase same here Bob," or "Select cat through dog" to choose exact words; if there is more than one match, hear its number, paragraph, and brief sentence context. Say "Next" or "Previous," then "That one," "Confirm that," or "OK." "Read sentence" repeats the context. "First match," "Second match," and "Match 3" still work. "Cancel selection" leaves the text unchanged. You can also say "Selection start at cat," then "Selection end at dog." Say "What is selected" to hear it. Say "Bold cat," "Underline cat," "Italicize cat," or "Highlight cat" for those words. Say "Copy cat," "Cut cat," "Delete cat," or "Delete the sentence I will be there" to edit named text. Say "Paste" to put copied text at the selection or end of the current paragraph. Say "Delete current word," "Delete line," or "Delete paragraph" for the current place. Say "Scratch" or "Scratch that" to delete selected text. When nothing is selected, these commands remove your most recent dictated addition. Say "Undo" to restore the deletion or reverse the last edit. Review the paragraph after any change.
Say "Undo" to reverse the last change during this session. If you notice a problem after reopening the app, ask your trainer for help with the saved recovery copies.
Say "Heading 1" through "Heading 6," or "Apply heading 1" through "Apply heading 6" to set a heading level. Say "Make this a heading" for level one, or "Normal text" to remove a heading. Say "Make this bold," "Make this italic," or "Underline this" to format the whole paragraph. Say "Remove bold," "Remove italic," or "Remove underline" to turn those off. Say "Set font to Arial" or "Set font size to 14 points" to change selected text, or the current paragraph when nothing is selected. Alignment, heading, list, and line-spacing commands apply to selected paragraphs when there is a selection. Say "Align left," "Align center," "Align right," or "Align justified" to position the paragraph. Say "Say font" or "Read formatting" for the full summary: font, size, style, bold, italic, underline, alignment, and line spacing. For one setting, say "Say font name," "Say size," "Say line spacing," "Say alignment," or "Say heading." Selected text with different settings is announced as mixed. Say "Set size 14" for a short size command. Say "Set font Arial 14" or "Set font Times New Roman size 16" to set both in one command. These commands also work in email bodies and dictation-only mode. For one word, say "Next word" or "Previous word" until you hear the word, then say "Bold word," "Italic word," "Underline word," "Set word font to Arial," or "Set word size to 14 points." Say "Remove bold from word," "Remove italic from word," or "Remove underline from word" to undo that formatting.
To change the space between lines, say "Single space" or "Double space." For another value, say "Custom spacing." The app asks what you want; say a number such as "One point seven five" or "Exactly eighteen points." The app repeats the value. Say "Yes" only when it is right. Say "No" to give another value, or "Cancel" to leave the spacing unchanged. You can also say "Line spacing 1.5" directly. Spacing changes the current paragraph, and the next new paragraph keeps it. Say "Set document line spacing to 1.25" to change every paragraph. Say "Read line spacing" to check the current paragraph, or "Read document line spacing" to check the whole document. Say "Reset line spacing" for the current paragraph, or "Reset document line spacing" for all paragraphs.
Say "Bulleted list" or "Numbered list" for the current paragraph. Say "New paragraph" for the next list item; say "End list" on that item to return to normal text.
Say "Create table with two rows and two columns." Speak to fill the first cell. Say "Next cell," "Previous cell," "Next row," "Previous row," "Next column," or "Previous column" to move. Say "Add row" or "Add column" to expand the table beside your current cell. Say "Delete row" or "Delete column" to remove one; say "Undo" immediately to restore it. Say "Read cell" or "Read table" to review. Say "Clear cell" to remove its words, "Leave table" to write after it, or "Delete table" to remove it. Say "Undo" immediately if a table was deleted accidentally. This test editor handles simple rectangular tables.
Say "Save document" to save under the current filename. Say "Save as My Letter" or "Save document as My Letter" to save under a new filename while keeping the original. It also saves as you write.
To name or rename the actual file, say "Name document My Letter." The name is not inserted into the body. To save a separate copy and keep the original, say "Save as My Letter"; "Save document as My Letter" also works. The app announces the saved .docx filename. If that filename already exists, choose another name. Write any title you want in the body separately. Say "Type literally" before body text that would otherwise be a naming command. Say "Undo" immediately after a rename to restore the previous filename.
Say "Leave document" to answer the save question and return to the main menu. After saving, say "Resume document" to return to it while the program remains open. Choosing No discards it from the current session.
To return to the main menu, say "Go back," "Exit document," or "Leave document," then answer Yes or No to saving. Yes asks for the filename; No discards changes. You can also say "Write an email" or "Write a note" directly. The document is saved before the new task starts.
Documents are saved on this computer as Word-compatible .docx files in your Windows Documents folder, inside Voice Companion. Say "Open documents" to show them in File Explorer, or say "Where are my documents?" to hear the location. Older documents are copied there without deleting the originals. You can create them without Word installed. After you open the program again, say "Wake up," then "List documents." Say "Open document My Letter" to return to a saved document by name. This test can reopen simple documents it created. It may refuse a Word document with complex formatting so it does not damage that file.

WRITE AN EMAIL DRAFT

To connect email, say "Connect my Gmail," "Connect my Outlook," "Connect my Yahoo," or "Add account" in the normal app. It asks which type: Gmail, Outlook or Microsoft 365 Exchange, or Yahoo. Google and Microsoft open their normal sign-in in a browser; a trusted person can assist. Yahoo asks for an app password in a private keyboard entry window; a helper needs to create that password in Yahoo Account Security. Never say passwords or codes aloud. If setup says sign-in is not available, ask your helper to complete the one-time provider registration described in the helper guide.
Say "Add another account" for another mailbox. Say "List email accounts" to hear each address. Say "Switch to Yahoo" or "Switch to Outlook" if there is only one such account. If there are two of the same type, say "Switch to" followed by the full email address. "Which email account?" reads the selection.
From the main menu, say "Email" or "Check email" to open the selected inbox. While reading the mailbox, say "New message" to write an email. From a draft, say "Cancel," "Cancel draft," "Go back," or "Back to inbox" to discard it and return to Inbox without another save. These commands work from every field in new, reply, reply-all, and forward drafts, even in dictation-only mode. New drafts and their automatic save copies are removed; if you opened a previously saved draft, its original saved version remains. A previous send attempt cannot be undone by canceling; its local record is retained. Say "Write an email" to create a draft from the selected account. Listen to the sending address before confirming any message.
When you start a new email, Companion asks, "Who do you want to send the email to?" Say the address, say a saved contact name, or type either into the Voice Companion window and press Enter. It then asks for the subject. Say the subject or type it and press Enter. Next it asks what the message should say. Speak naturally or type into the same window and press Enter. To return to a field, say "Go to To," "Go to Subject," "Go to CC," "Go to BCC," or "Go to Body." Companion tells you the current value and asks for the replacement or added address. You may also say "Email to..." or "Subject is..." directly.
Say "CC" or "BCC" at any point in an email draft to add an optional copy recipient. Say or type one address, or say "Skip." Say "Read CC" or "Read BCC" to check. A BCC recipient is included in the private sending review.
Say "Add contact Bob as bob@example.com" or type that command and press Enter. Say "List contacts" or "Find contact Bob" to check the address book. Contacts are kept separately for each selected email account. With Gmail selected, say "Connect Google Contacts" or "Sync Google Contacts" to open the normal Google permission screen and exchange contacts. A trusted helper can complete that permission step. Saved local contacts remain available without internet.
To type or correct an address, say "Go to To," "Go to CC," or "Go to BCC." The typing box becomes ready automatically. When returning to To, your current address appears for editing. Left and Right Arrow review characters aloud; Backspace and Delete remove characters and announce the deletion. New characters are spoken as you type using your local synthesizer: eSpeak when selected, otherwise Windows speech, including when an AI narration voice is selected. Quick Test opens the typing window too. Press Enter to save the corrected address. When a new email opens, To is ready automatically too. The To field can also be edited in forward, reply, and reply-all drafts; after recording an address in a response draft, Companion returns to the message body. Repeated prompts do not erase an address you are typing. Alt+T is only a fallback if another window has taken keyboard focus. Say "Email to friend@example.com" with the full email address. Say "Read recipient" to check it.
You may say "Email to friend at example dot com." The app repeats the address it recorded; check it carefully before using the draft.
Say "Subject is Hello from me." Say "Read subject" to check it.
These same select, find, bold, underline, italicize, highlight, copy, cut, paste, delete, and scratch that commands work in the email message body. Font, font size, alignment, headings one through six, line spacing, bulleted lists, numbered lists, and tables also work there, including in dictation-only mode. Use the document commands above. Formatting is preserved in saved drafts and outgoing Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo messages, including replies and forwards. The receiving email program may display fonts and spacing differently. Selection commands also work in To, CC, BCC, and Subject; these fields contain plain text. In the typing window, selection uses your current entry, including characters you have not submitted yet. Say "What is selected" to hear it. Type to replace selected text, then press Enter to record the field.
After the recipient and subject, just speak your message. The app adds ordinary speech to the draft and handles short commands such as "Read paragraph," "Undo," and "New paragraph." You do not need to start or stop dictation. The app turns spoken "period," "comma," "question mark," and "exclamation point" into punctuation marks within or at the end of an utterance, including when the next word is lowercase and removes a stray final "uh" or "um." Listen back before sending. To put exact command or punctuation words into your message, say "Type literally" followed by those words. Say "Save draft" when finished. Say "Cancel" or "Back to inbox" to discard the draft. Say "Save draft" to keep it before leaving; "Exit" or "Leave email" saves it and returns to the main menu.
You can say "Create a document" or "Write a note" directly while in an email draft. The draft is saved before the new task starts.
Later, say "List email drafts" and "Open email draft Untitled" (or the draft name you heard) to return to one after restarting.
If it says a saved draft could not be opened, ask your trainer for help. The app leaves that saved file untouched.
IMPORTANT: Ask your helper to connect and test an account before relying on email. Without a connected account, "Send email" saves a local draft and does NOT send it. With a connected account, say "Send it" or "Send that." Listen to the sender, recipient, subject, and whole message. Say "Cancel send" if anything is wrong. Say "Yes" only when all of it is correct. Nothing is sent before that review and confirmation. After the provider accepts it, the app first says "Email sent successfully" without reading technical reference codes. It finishes that announcement before refreshing the folder you were using. For a reply or forward, it returns to the original message if that message is still on the refreshed page; otherwise it returns to the folder list. Ask your helper to verify delivery during early tests. If the app says the result is uncertain, check with the recipient and your mail account before trying again.

WEB BROWSING

Say "Search the web for the weather." Voice Companion searches with Brave Search in its guided browser and reads the page in short pieces. You can say "Google weather" to try Google first. If a search engine asks you to prove you are human or does not provide readable results, Companion says so and tries another search engine. It cannot solve a verification challenge for you; ask your trainer if every search page is blocked. Say "Continue reading" repeatedly to hear the rest of a long page. "Read page" begins again at the top. Say "Read page," or "What does this page say about tomorrow?" You can ask a follow-up search with "What about Sunday?" Search results and pages may be wrong or incomplete; check the source before acting.
If you cannot find a link, say "List links." The app checks links in the page, open shadow content, and embedded frames, then waits briefly for late results. If it still cannot identify them, ask your trainer to check that site. It reads one numbered link at a time. Say "Next" or "Previous" to move through links, or "Open link 1" (also "Open link number one"). The app repeats the chosen link and site. Say "Yes," "Okay," "Confirm that," or "That one" to open it; say "No" to hear the next link. You may also say "Next link" or "Previous link" one at a time, then "Okay" on the one you want. Say "Open website example dot com." Listen to the address, then say "Yes" to open it or "No" to cancel. Voice Companion reads the page in its own browser window. Within settings, say a category name, "Next setting," "Previous setting," "Next," "Previous," "Read setting," "On," "Off," "Toggle," "OK," or "Cancel." Say setting names followed by values, such as "Verbosity medium," "Punctuation most," "Rate 4," "Volume 50," "Default font Times New Roman," or "Default line spacing double." Settings remain staged until OK. Buttons that open another area close settings and discard unsaved edits.
Say "Verbosity high," "Verbosity medium," or "Verbosity low" outside settings to save that choice immediately. High reads the complete application guidance, including command hints. At the main menu it says: Main menu. What would you like to do? Say next or previous to move through the items, and okay, confirm, confirm that, or that one to open. Medium says: Main menu. What would you like to do? Say next or previous, and OK to open. In other guidance prompts, Medium keeps the main message and the first command-hint sentence. Low says only Main menu at the main menu and removes the Say command-hint portion from other guidance prompts. These presets affect marked application guidance; they do not shorten document or email content, errors, confirmation results, or setting names and values. Punctuation, typing echo, and delayed phonetics are separate choices in Verbosity and are not changed by a verbosity preset.
Say "Punctuation none," "Punctuation some," "Punctuation most," or "Punctuation all." Most speaks more symbols than Some while leaving periods as natural pauses; All also speaks periods. This changes speech, not stored punctuation.

KEYBOARD NAVIGATION

Use Up or Left for Previous, Down or Right for Next, and Enter to open or confirm menu and list choices. In an email list, Enter opens the highlighted message and reads its body from the beginning. Escape goes back or cancels the current choice. In an email draft Escape discards the draft. In a document or email body, type directly. Enter creates a new paragraph; typed words are literal text, so typing period or new line keeps those words. Left and Right move by character and immediately speak the character at the new caret. Control+Left and Control+Right move by word. Up and Down move by line, Control+Up and Control+Down move by paragraph, Home and End move within the line, and Control+Home and Control+End move to the beginning or end of the text. Hold Shift with movement keys to select text; Control+A selects all. These keyboard controls continue working while the microphone sleeps. While choosing a matching selection or replacement, arrows browse matches, Enter confirms, and Escape cancels that choice. Returning to a menu restores menu-key focus automatically. Control+Comma opens settings.

TYPING ECHO AND PHONETICS

In Control+Comma, choose Verbosity and Tab to Typing echo. Characters speaks each inserted character; Words speaks a completed word when you type a space or Enter; Characters and words speaks both; None turns off typing feedback. Cursor navigation still speaks with any typing-echo setting. Say Typing echo words, Typing echo characters, Typing echo characters and words, or Typing echo none to save the choice outside settings. Feedback uses your local eSpeak or Windows voice, including when AI narration is selected.
In Verbosity, Delayed phonetic pronunciation is enabled by default. After Left or Right lands on a letter, the letter speaks immediately and its phonetic name follows after half a second, for example B followed by Bravo. Another key or focus change cancels the pending phonetic name. Toggle this checkbox off or choose a delay of 0.5, 1, or 2 seconds. Say Phonetics off, Phonetics on, or Phonetic delay 1 second outside settings. Phonetics applies to caret review, not every typed letter.

Shut up and Pause also pause keyboard speech. Resume continues it. Silencing speech cancels a phonetic hint that has not yet spoken. Moving or typing again gives fresh feedback.

KEYBOARD EMAIL READING AND DOCUMENT NAMING

Opening a message reads its body and focuses a read-only text view. Use the same character, word, line, paragraph, and selection keys as in a document to review the received message. Copying selected text is allowed; typing cannot change received mail. Escape returns to the folder list, where arrow keys move through messages again.
When you leave a document using Escape, main menu, close, or leave document, Companion asks whether to save it. Say Yes or No, or use arrow keys to choose Yes or No and press Enter. Yes starts the document name field, including for an already named document. Say the name to fill the field, then press Enter or say Okay; alternatively type the name and press Enter. The name is the filename, never body text. No discards changes since opening or your last explicit save or naming command; an existing file is restored, and a new unsaved file is removed. Escape at the save question or name field keeps editing. Press F2 while writing to name or rename the file; type or say the name and press Enter. F2 naming keeps you in the document afterward.

KEYBOARD MAIL AND EXIT
The main menu includes Exit Voice Companion. Say close companion, exit companion, or return to Windows to close the app. Alt+F4 does the same. If a document is open, the save question appears first.
In email, Control+N starts a new message; Control+R replies; Control+Shift+R replies to all; Control+F forwards; Control+Y chooses a folder; F5 refreshes the list. Arrows move through folder and message lists and Enter opens the choice.
In the message list, Space toggles the current message selection; Shift+Up and Shift+Down extend selection while moving; Control+A selects messages on the current loaded page. Delete deletes the current or selected messages after confirmation. Control+Shift+V chooses a destination to move them, then asks for confirmation. Use arrows to choose Yes or No and Enter to answer; Escape cancels. Inside a received message, Control+A selects its text for copying instead of selecting messages.
F2 in email starts Rename folder: choose the folder with arrows and Enter, then type the new name and press Enter or say rename to followed by the new name. Control+Shift+D starts Delete folder. Only empty user-created folders without children can be deleted; standard provider folders remain protected. Folder changes require Yes or No confirmation. Control+Shift+E starts Create folder; type create folder followed by its name and press Enter. Voice commands create folder, rename folder, and delete folder remain available.
In a draft, Control+Enter requests sending; the usual confirmation still applies. Escape cancels the draft. Editing text keeps the standard character, word, line, paragraph, selection, copy, and paste shortcuts.
Across areas, arrows, Enter, Escape, and Control+Comma provide navigation and settings, including in Dictation Only mode. Document exit commands still offer the save question in that mode. You may also type any available voice command in the command field and press Enter; Alt+T focuses that field, so every app command has a keyboard path.


RELEASE NOTES AND BRAILLE
Each installer includes the current manual and detailed release notes in TXT, HTML, DOCX, EPUB, text-only DAISY 3, and BRF. All editions of each publication come from the same source. The BRF copies use contracted Unified English Braille, 40 cells per line, and 25 lines per page. They are mechanically translated and have not been human Braille proofread. The app does not require a Braille translator to read its guides.
Find the files under Documents, Voice Companion, User Guides. Say open user guides, or press Alt+T, type open user guides, and press Enter. Updates replace the shipped copies with the current build editions and preserve personal files in this folder. Documentation files are also separate downloads on each new GitHub release.
Release notes follow the supplied reference structure: a level 1 release title, level 2 New Apps and Features and Fixes and Enhancements headings, numbered topics, and lettered details. Numbering starts over for each main section, and lettering starts over for each topic. TXT displays the same numbers and letters without heading styles. Official releases must have comprehensive notes describing actual changes since the named comparison release. Testing builds are labeled as testing builds.
The current email shortcuts are Ctrl+N for new email, Ctrl+R for reply, Ctrl+Shift+R for reply all, Ctrl+F for forward, Ctrl+Y for folders, F5 for refresh, Ctrl+Enter in a draft for send with confirmation, and Escape in a draft for cancel without saving.
