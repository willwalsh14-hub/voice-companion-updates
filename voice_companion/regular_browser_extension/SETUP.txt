VOICE COMPANION BROWSER CONNECTION
Testing build 0.2.144-test

One-time setup:
1. Install this update and open Voice Companion.
2. In Chrome or Edge, open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
3. Turn on Developer mode. Choose Load unpacked.
4. Select %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Voice Companion\regular_browser_extension.
If you loaded an older version, choose Reload on its extension card once.
Its name is Voice Companion Browser Connection. Its ID stays the same.
The installer sets up the local host. No Python or commands are needed.

Use your usual Web browser menu:
The extension links automatically when Companion is open. You do not need to
open its popup or connect each tab or site. New tabs link on their own. After
a browser or app restart, it tries again on its own within about 30 seconds.
Open Web browser in Companion and choose Resume where you left off. When you
switch browser tabs, Companion reads the active website tab. Search, favorites,
history, Back, Forward, element keys, arrow reading, lists, tables, typing,
dictation and forms keep the same menu and commands.

The popup is only for status or pausing the browser connection. Pausing is
saved until you choose Enable browser connection. Your tabs stay open.
The browser shows its normal debugging notice. Settings pages are not linked.
Site security checks still apply. PayPal sign-in remains unverified. Human
verification must still be completed by the user; Companion does not force a
verification box to stay checked or solve the challenge for you.
