VOICE COMPANION - SET UP AN AI VOICE

1. Before you start
An AI voice reads text through an online speech service. Windows voices are the default. You can keep using them without setting up an AI voice.
AI speech needs internet access and a separate paid service account. A ChatGPT plan does not cover this app's speech use.
The text being read may include your email and documents. It is sent to OpenAI only when you allow this in the settings. Decide whether you want that before saving.

2. Add the service key
A service key is a private code that lets the app use your speech account. Ask someone you trust for help if needed.
1. Open Options.
2. Choose Synthesizer options.
3. Type or paste your key into the private AI API key box.
4. Choose an AI voice.
5. Check Allow narration text to be sent to OpenAI only if you agree.
6. Choose OK to save.
A blank key box keeps the saved key. Cancel or Escape leaves your changes unsaved.
The saved key is protected by your Windows user account. Do not read it aloud or put it in a shared file.

3. Choose and test a voice
1. Say "List voices".
2. Use "Next" or "Previous" to choose AI voice.
3. Say "Okay".
4. Use "Next voice" or "Previous voice" to hear a sample.
5. Say "Okay" to keep the voice.
Say "Cancel voice" to restore your earlier choice. The voice choice is saved for the next time you open the app.
After setup, "Use AI voice" switches to AI speech. "Use SAPI" switches to Windows speech. "Use eSpeak" chooses the other local speech system.
Try a short passage first. Check that it sounds clear through the speaker or headset you plan to use.

4. Stop or change speech
Control stops speech. "Be quiet" pauses it. "Resume speaking" continues it.
A new action stops the old spoken message. Late audio from that message is ignored.
Say "Faster", "Slower", "Louder", or "Quieter" to change speech. "Rate 2" sets speed. "Volume 50" sets volume to fifty percent.
Long passages may take longer to start. Quick typing and movement messages use a local voice so keys can be heard promptly.
If the online service fails, Companion tries a local voice for the text.

5. Remove a saved key
1. Open Options, then Synthesizer options.
2. Check Remove saved AI key.
3. Choose OK.
Use "Use SAPI" to return to Windows speech.
If a support person set a key outside the app, ask them to remove that separate setting too.
Removing the key from Companion does not close your speech service account.

6. Check before regular use
Try pause, resume, a new command, and a lost internet connection. Check that local speech still works.
Check the service account's charges. Audio is played from memory; the app does not save it as a recording.
For more help, read chapter 4 of the user guide.
